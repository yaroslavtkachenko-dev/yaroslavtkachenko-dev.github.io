PicShift support & privacy

PicShift is a batch image converter for macOS. It converts, resizes and strips metadata from photos entirely on your own Mac.

Get help Purchases & refunds Privacy policy

Get help

If something does not work the way you expected, write to me directly. One person answers this address, usually the same day and within two business days at the outside.

tkachenko.ya@icloud.com

What to include

These four lines turn a bug report into a fix, and usually save a round trip:

  • Your macOS version
  • The source file format, and the format you converted to
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • Whether it affects every file or just one particular image

Please do not attach photos you would rather not share. A description of the file is normally enough, and I will ask if a sample is genuinely needed.

Purchases & refunds

The free tier includes 70 photo conversions. PicShift Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited conversions. There is no subscription and no account to create.

Bought it and Pro is not showing up on a second Mac, or after reinstalling? Use Restore Purchases, in the PicShift menu or on the purchase screen. It re-checks your Apple receipt and turns Pro back on.

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple, not by me. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. I never see your payment details.

Privacy policy

PicShift does not collect any data.

No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no network calls.

That is the entire policy. The detail below is only there to say precisely what it means.

Your images

  • Every conversion runs locally, on your own Mac.
  • Your images are never uploaded anywhere. There is nowhere for them to go.
  • PicShift reads only the files and the output folder you pick yourself, by drag-and-drop or through the file picker.
  • It asks for no other permissions, and holds no access to the rest of your disk.

The network

The app contains no networking code at all. It does not phone home, does not check for updates on its own, and has no analytics or crash-reporting service embedded in it. Updates arrive through the Mac App Store, like any other app you install there.

What is stored on your Mac

  • Your settings: output format, quality, resize choice.
  • The output folder you last used, so you do not have to pick it again.
  • A count of how many files you have converted, which is what the free tier is measured against.

All three stay on your machine. None of them is sent anywhere, and none of them identifies you.

Purchases

The purchase itself is processed by Apple through the Mac App Store. PicShift asks Apple whether a valid purchase exists and gets back yes or no. It never receives your name, your email or your payment information.

Children

PicShift collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the date below changes with it, and the change is described in the release notes of the version that introduces it.
