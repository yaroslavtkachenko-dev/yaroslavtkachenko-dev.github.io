FGH support & privacy

FGH is a menu bar app for Mac that cleans the text you copy: formatting, tracking parameters and the invisible characters some AI tools leave behind. Everything happens on your own Mac. FGH is in development and not on sale yet.

Get help

If something does not work the way you expected, write to me directly. One person answers this address, usually the same day and within two business days at the outside.

tkachenko.ya@icloud.com

What to include

  • Your macOS version, and the FGH version (right-click the FGH icon in the menu bar and choose About FGH)

  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead

Privacy policy

FGH does not collect any data.

No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no network calls.

That is the entire policy. The detail below is only there to say precisely what it means.

What FGH reads

  • The text you copy, at the moment you copy it, so that it can be cleaned. This happens in memory, on your Mac.
  • FGH leaves alone anything that apps mark as concealed, transient or auto-generated, the convention password managers use, and anything copied from 1Password, Bitwarden, LastPass, Dashlane, KeePassXC, Proton Pass, NordPass, RoboForm or Enpass.
  • Images and files are ignored.

Clipboard history

History is off until you turn it on. When it is on, FGH keeps up to the last 100 text items and deletes each one after 72 hours. The history is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored in your Mac’s Keychain. The key is created on your Mac and never leaves it.

Permissions

  • Accessibility, only if you grant it: it lets ⌥⌘1–9 paste a history item straight into the app you are using. Without it, FGH puts the item on the clipboard and you paste it yourself.
  • Launch at Login, only if you turn it on in Settings.

FGH runs in Apple’s app sandbox and asks for nothing else.

The network

The app contains no networking code at all, and the sandbox gives it no network access. It does not phone home, does not check for updates on its own, and has no analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party service embedded in it.

What is stored on your Mac

  • Your settings, such as which cleaning options are on and whether history is on.
  • The clipboard history and its encryption key, in the Keychain, only while history is on.

None of it is sent anywhere, and none of it identifies you.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes the clipboard history, its encryption key and all your settings at once. Moving the app to the Trash does not clear the Keychain, so use Delete all data first if you want nothing left behind.

Children

FGH collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
